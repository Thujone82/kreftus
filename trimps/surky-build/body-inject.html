Quick Workflow & Field Legend

1. Import Save
In Trimps, click Export to copy your save string, then press V or click Paste Save. The save box automatically collapses once loaded (click Update Save or press U anytime to view or edit it).
2. Choose Preset
Select a preset on the right (e.g., Easy Radon Challenge, Tough Radon Challenge, Push/C^3/Mayhem, Alchemy, Trappa, etc.) and review the weight inputs.
3. Optimize & Copy
Click Autobuy (respec available) or press A, then click Copy Perks (C) — or press Shift+A to optimize and copy to clipboard in a single step.
Green Inputs
Required user inputs (target zone, weights, coord-limited). Presets populate suggested weights, which you can fine-tune.
Blue Inputs
Automatically populated when importing any U1 or U2 save (Radon for perks, Runetrinkets).
Red Inputs
Populated from a Universe 2 save near the end of your run (Rn/run, weapon/armor levels, tributes, meteorologists, collectors, smithies). Check Lock "red" inputs before importing a mid-run or U1 save.
