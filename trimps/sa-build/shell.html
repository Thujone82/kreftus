Glossary

Runnable Build
A community sheet build where you already own every required item at or above the listed level, have enough Extra Limbs slots, and meet any Ring or Contract requirements.
Next Upgrade
Prioritizes any single atomic upgrade (1 item level, 1 limb, 1 ring level, or a reachable contract) that immediately unlocks a higher dust/s sheet build. Otherwise picks the first step on the fewest-step path.
Shards vs Dust
Late-game SA currency where 1 Shard = 1,000,000,000 (1B) Dust. Wait times convert Shards to Dust automatically against your active dust/s rate.
Contracts
Unlocked by completing a Void Map at or above the item's U2 Zone requirement after purchasing the contract in Spire Assault.
Payback
Total remaining upgrade cost divided by your current dust/s rate, showing how long it takes to farm the missing pieces for a better build.
