Knucklebones v1.0

Knucklebones is a cross-platform dice game from Cult of the Lamb, written in Go with tcell and Ebitengine. The terminal UI is the primary mode; a matching graphical window opens when launched with -gui or double-clicked in Explorer. Two players face each other across 3×3 grids, rolling a die each turn and placing it into one of three columns. Matching dice multiply in value, while placing a die destroys matching dice in the opponent's facing column. Play against three AI opponents (Ratau, Flinky, and Shrumy), against a friend on the same keyboard, or over LAN/TCP network multiplayer.

Controls

Your Turn: ← → or A/D to select a column (or hover/click in GUI). Place the rolled die with Enter, Space, or digit keys 1–3. When the two-dice house rule is active, toggle the active die with Tab or Q/E.

Menus: ↑ ↓ or W/S to navigate options, Enter or Space to select, T to toggle Dark/Light theme, and Esc to quit.

In-Game Actions: Press L for the full move log, H for match history, R to resign (with confirmation), Esc to return to the menu, and V to toggle the result panel to inspect the final board.

Mouse (GUI Window): Directly click any menu button, column, tray die, or control button beneath the board.

Features

  • Dual Launch Modes — Plays natively in modern terminal consoles (TUI via tcell with Unicode box-drawing & Cascadia Mono support) or as a resizable desktop window (GUI via Ebitengine).
  • Three AI Personalities — Ratau (Easy, random placements), Flinky (Medium, greedy immediate swing scoring), and Shrumy (Hard, lookahead scoring and endgame awareness).
  • Online LAN / TCP Multiplayer — Host or join direct peer-to-peer network duels on configurable ports with live placement aim previews, turn timers (15/30/60s), and 30-second disconnect grace periods.
  • Interactive Placement Preview — Selecting or hovering a column previews the landing slot, highlights opponent dice marked for destruction in red, and computes real-time score swings.
  • Configurable House Rules — Play Best of 1, 3, or 5 rounds, disable column destruction, roll two dice and pick one, or play until both boards are full.
  • Persistent Statistics & Top 10 — Automatically records match records across AI tiers, local hotseat, and online play, preserving a Top 10 scoreboard in knucklebones.json.
  • Dark & Light Themes — 24-bit TrueColor visuals with single (ivory), pair (gold), and triple (blue) die multipliers.
