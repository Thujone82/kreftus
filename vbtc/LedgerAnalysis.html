Glossary

Cost basis
FIFO (oldest lots first), LIFO (newest first), HIFO (highest cost first), or AVG (weighted average cost).
Realized P/L
Sell proceeds minus cost basis of the BTC sold under the active accounting method.
Unrealized P/L
Open buy lots valued at the latest rate minus their cost basis.
Total P/L
Cumulative realized + unrealized at each trade. KPI shows change over the selected range.
vs. HODL
Active Total P/L minus passive Buy & Hold P/L on the same capital deployed.
Sharpe / Sortino
Annualized risk-adjusted daily P/L return vs. total volatility (Sharpe) or downside volatility (Sortino).
Effective price
Trade USD ÷ trade BTC — the price actually paid or received.
Win rate
Share of sells with positive realized P/L (break-even excluded).
Profit factor
Gross realized gains ÷ gross realized losses.
Expectancy
Average realized P/L per sell.
Max drawdown
Largest drop in Total P/L from a running peak within the range, plus time underwater.
Hold time
BTC-weighted age of the buy lots consumed by a sell.
Unmatched sell
BTC sold with no prior buy in the loaded data (e.g. missing history); excluded from P/L.
Continuity gap
User BTC differs from previous User BTC ± trade BTC by more than 2 satoshis.
