Larry v1.5

Larry is a console arcade game where you guide a bright highlight ⬢ across roads with moving traffic. Levels add a fresh color theme, more cars, and faster lanes. Safe shoulders and gaps offer breathing room; from level 6, impassable ☙ debris appears on mid safe gaps; from level 8, a flashing ♥ on a mid safe lane grants an extra life; from level 10, a flashing ♦ on the top mid safe lane grants +1,000 points. Earn points for each row climbed and for reaching the far side (marked with a ▚▞ goal pattern).

Controls

Start menu: ↑↓ hop Larry, Enter to confirm (Start / High Scores / Quit). In game: Arrows or WASD to move, Space to pause. Esc opens a confirm dialog where Larry hops between Yes and No; Esc on the main menu exits.

Features

  • Start menu with Larry hop cursor and High Scores access
  • Animated traffic backdrop behind the title screen
  • WASD/Arrow controls, Space to pause with overlay
  • Esc confirm: Larry hops Yes/No to return to the main menu
  • Safe shoulders, random safe gaps, and a ▚▞ goal checker
  • UTF-8 vehicles: motorcycles (═/▩), cars (█/◙), trucks (█/▓) with ▶/◀
  • Level-based color themes, density/speed scaling (levels continue past 9)
  • From level 6: impassable ☙ debris on mid safe gaps (ramps to 10% cap)
  • From level 8: flashing mid-lane ♥ life pickup with inverted HUD +1 Life notice
  • From level 10: flashing top mid-lane ♦ score pickup with inverted HUD +1,000 Points notice
  • Lives, per-row scoring (+10, or +level from L11), session Top, and Best from history
  • Top-10 scoreboard: hop Larry to browse runs and view saved details (death level, hearts/gems, version)
  • Auto-requests 80×42 console size; resize-aware rendering
