Sudoku Solver & OCR v1.0

Sudoku Solver & OCR is a cross-platform desktop Sudoku application written in Go with Ebitengine. Fill the 9×9 grid interactively, paste a screenshot of any puzzle to have OCR automatically extract and recognize the givens, or paste puzzle text in a variety of common formats. The built-in backtracking solver completes any legal puzzle instantly while rejecting boards with conflicting givens. The app includes 1,000 curated puzzles sampled from the public-domain Sudoku Exchange Puzzle Bank across Easy, Medium, Hard, and Diabolical tiers.

Controls

Board: Click a cell or navigate with arrow keys (wraps at board edges). Enter digits 1–9 with number keys or numpad. Clear a cell with 0, Backspace, or Delete.

Keypad: Click digit buttons 1–9 or "Erase Selected Cell" on the right panel.

Buttons & Paste: Solve Puzzle solves and completes the rest of a legal board in green. Clear Grid empties all cells. Paste Screenshot (Ctrl+V) reads a puzzle image from the clipboard with animated OCR progress, or parses copied text grids and puzzle strings.

Difficulty Die: Left-click the die in the header to load a random puzzle of the active difficulty. Right-click cycles the difficulty tier and die color (green Easy, yellow Medium, red Hard, purple Diabolical). Hovering displays quick helper text.

Features

  • Interactive 9×9 Board — Click or arrow keys to select cells; entered givens appear in gold, solver solutions in green.
  • Backtracking Solver — Completes legal puzzles instantly; conflicting givens are rejected with clear error feedback.
  • Optical Character Recognition (OCR) — Paste screenshot (Ctrl+V or button); automated lattice line detection and digit glyph recognition with animated progress.
  • Flexible Text Paste — Automatically reads 81-character puzzle strings (e.g. 60005...009 with 0, ., _, -, *, or x for blanks), 9×9 multi-line grids, ASCII box-drawing tables, CSV/TSV, and 1D/2D arrays.
  • Difficulty Die & Puzzle Bank — Header die with color feedback; 250 puzzles each for Easy, Medium, Hard, and Diabolical (1,000 total) from Sudoku Exchange Puzzle Bank.
  • Mouseover Tooltip — Dynamic two-line helper text (Click for <Difficulty> Puzzle / Right Click Change Difficulty) appears beside the die.
  • On-screen Keypad — Clean dark-slate UI with 1–9 digit keypad and Erase Selected Cell button.
  • Desktop GUI Subsystem — Cross-platform Go binary with embedded Windows icon, launching directly as a window without an extra console prompt.
